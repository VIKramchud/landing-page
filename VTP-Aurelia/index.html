About VTP Aurelia New Kharadi

VTP Aurelia is a landmark taking shape in New Kharadi, Pune – a graceful departure from the ordinary that brings classical European-style design to the skyline. With tall, ornate towers, delicately arched windows, majestic promenades and serene courtyards, the project offers elegant 2 and 3 BHK residences, including simplex and duplex layouts, just minutes from EON IT Park and the World Trade Center. Set next to the BAIF forest within a township of 8000+ families, every home is planned for privacy, open views and lush greenery, with premium finishes and flexible payment plans for easy ownership.

About Developer

VTP Realty.

VTP Aurelia is developed by VTP Realty, Pune's leading real estate brand with over 35 years of expertise in construction. VTP Realty has earned a reputation as a turnaround specialist by successfully reviving large-scale projects such as Codename Township Pegasus in East Pune and Codename Township Blue Waters in West Pune. With strong backward integration, VTP sources nearly 70% of its construction materials from group companies such as VTP Materials and Viraj Projects, ensuring superior quality and faster project delivery at New Kharadi.
